# Explorer brand assets

- `FreeCAD.svg` is the official FreeCAD application logo from
  `FreeCAD/FreeCAD/src/Gui/Icons/freecad.svg`. It is used only to identify
  FreeCAD documents. FreeCAD documents that the logo is available under the
  source-code license and that its trademark may be used to reference FreeCAD.
- `VTK.svg` is the official VTK wordmark used only to identify VTK data files.
  VTK publishes its logo files for recognition on its official About page.

FreeCAD and VTK are trademarks of their respective owners. Their inclusion
does not imply endorsement of PyEMSI.
